# P-012 远程工作可见性缺失

## 问题描述

远程工作里最难的不是干活，是"communicating what I was working on with my
team"：不在工位上，别人看不到你在推进什么，自己则"like I was in my own
little bubble"，被怀疑摸鱼或被边缘化。

## 证据

- HN「Ask HN: Remote workers, how do you show your work?」（105 points /
  45 comments），OP："one of the hardest problems was communicating what I
  was working on with my team."；dalemyers："I always felt like I was behind
  the rest of the team since they could all see what each other was doing,
  but I felt like I was in my own little bubble."
  https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=16197364

## 现有方案为什么不足

可见性靠"汇报"这一人肉动作实现；工具本可以从工作流中自动沉淀可见进度，
却把负担压回给个人。

## 可能的解决方向

进度可见性自动化：从任务流转/提交/文档中生成"谁在推进什么"，汇报降级为补充。
