# P-095 自托管需求真空

## 问题描述

"希望自托管什么"的社区问答里，协作三件套齐声喊缺：Notion 平替
（"I'd love an Notion.so alternative, I love the service but I wish
the team plan was free"，两人要 $200+）；Jira 平替（"without paying
thousands of dollars for an enterprise subscription"，且试过的开源
替代都不如）；Miro 平替（"Tried several FOSS alternatives.. but..
not even close"）。与 P-017/P-006 相互印证：这不是单品缺失，
是整个生态位的真空。
