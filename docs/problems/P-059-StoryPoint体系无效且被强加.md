# P-059 Story Point 体系无效且被强加

## 问题描述

三连击：跨学科团队里点数黑话完全对不上（"Manhours = story points
Task = story Subtask = sprint..."，345 分热帖）；点数体系从来不是
团队自发的——"always foisted upon the team by managers… turn
estimates into promises"；为"3 点还是 5 点"争吵的每个小时本可以
拿来干活。

## 证据

- HN「Story points are pointless, measure queues」（345/288）：以上
  两条原文
- HN 44187642（28/42）："The whole story point thing seems like a
  scam where some invented currency is used to trick people into not
  seeing reality."
- 深挖补充（40969693 帖内）："nobody ever figured out a way to produce
  reliable estimates"（估算困境）；"It's also easy to game the system.
  Turn a couple of 3's into 5's. Your 'velocity' goes up"（数据操纵）；
  "Story points can enable corporate despotism"（权力工具化）。

## 可能的解决方向

估算回归真实单位（小时/天）且仅团队内部可见，不向上承诺化。
