# P-034 权限系统粗且易翻车

## 问题描述

两个方向同时失败：Confluence 权限树继承出故障时**静默越权**——
"child pages with sensitive information became viewable to every user"，
用户从此不敢往里放任何敏感内容；反过来 Kanboard 粒度太粗，成员被迫
看到全部任务，无法限制"只见分配给自己的卡"。

## 证据

- HN 18874707（102 分 / 76 评论），dimitar："permissions on pages are
  inherited in a tree-like manner… at some point child pages with
  sensitive information became viewable to every user... I would
  absolutely never rely on the permissions in Confluence to store
  anything sensitive in it ever again."
- Kanboard#5501："This is good for assigning confidential leads to team
  members so they cannot see the leads of the other users"（要求按成员
  限制可见范围）

## 现有方案为什么不够

权限是安全功能却被当普通功能测：继承故障不告警、越权无审计、
粒度只到"项目级"。

## 可能的解决方向

权限最小默认（need-to-know）+ 越权可见的审计日志 + 权限变更的
影响预览。
