# P-011 混合办公协作名存实亡

## 问题描述

混合办公的现实是"travel into an empty office and sit on Zoom calls anyway"——
花通勤进办公室，协作体验却仍是视频会议；同时会议文化退化：多人同时抢话、
公司强制全天开摄像头，高频会议把开发者一天切成碎片。

## 证据

- HN「Ask HN: Why don't I see gold at the end of the remote working rainbow?」
  （468 points / 787 comments，高热度），benjaminwootton："you travel into an
  empty office and sit on Zoom calls anyway... the experience of working
  together is effectively not available right now."；snarf21："Everyone has
  forgotten how to have a meeting outside of Zoom. 5 conversations at once,
  constant interruptions"（实测链接与热度一致）
  https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=33259351
- Reddit r/remotework："I can see how this would be hell for IT workers and
  developers since they get pulled into meetings all throughout the day"
  https://www.reddit.com/r/remotework/comments/1ccycev/companies_requiring_remote_employees_to_have/

## 现有方案为什么不足

会议软件优化的是音视频质量，混合协作缺的是"共同工作空间"（一起看板、
一起改文档、异步接续），这块没有产品化。
